Advice for families, investors and philanthropists

On schools, education businesses, giving and impact investment: what to choose, build, buy or fund, and how to know it is working.

Approach

Independent advice, tested against evidence

The market, the numbers and the educational case are tested before anyone is advised to commit capital, and the advice says so when the answer is no. Every recommendation can be traced to the evidence behind it.

"In private client work, be it education planning for families or in the context of investments, honesty, transparency and open dialogue are key."Matthew Goldie-Scot, Managing Director, speaking to Spear's

Philanthropy & impact

Giving and investing that can show what it achieved

Clear questions before money moves, honest measurement after, and practical advice on what to fund next.

Spear's Awards 2026Finalist, Philanthropy & Impact Advisory Firm of the Year

  • Giving strategy

    Landscape reviews and needs assessments to help a family or foundation decide where its money can make the most difference, through which partners and on what timescale.

  • Impact design

    Designing programmes and grants with the result in mind from the start: a clear theory of change, a results framework, and measures that will show whether it worked. Includes due diligence on grantees and partners before funds are committed.

  • Impact investing

    Due diligence on schools, education businesses and social enterprises that combines the commercial case with the evidence on outcomes, and impact measurement frameworks that investors can report against, with advice on outcomes-based financing, where payment follows verified results.

  • Monitoring, evaluation and learning

    Baselines, evaluations and learning studies that tell a funder what changed, for whom and why, and what to do differently next time.
