  3. October 2026Work

    Lower Saxony publishes its Framework for Quality Pedagogical Practice in Early Childhood Education and Care

    The Framework, funded by the European Union's Technical Support Instrument and implemented by UNICEF with the European Commission and the Lower Saxony Ministry of Education, has been published. Alexandra Tigan led the two studies that laid its foundations: an as-is analysis of early childhood education and care in Lower Saxony, and a good practice review of the Czech Republic, Denmark, England, Estonia and Scotland.

  5. October 2026Work

    Mid-term review of Uzbekistan's GPE Partnership Compact completed

    The mid-term review of Uzbekistan's Partnership Compact for education reform, led by the Ministry of Preschool and School Education, has been completed. Matthew Goldie-Scot facilitated the multi-stakeholder dialogue and presented the review memo to the Local Education Group, and continues to support Uzbekistan's reforms on behalf of UNICEF and the World Bank.

    LinkedIn

  6. September 2026Work

    Labour market assessment to shape vocational training for returnees and displaced people in Georgia

    For IOM Georgia's REforM project, the Thuso team assessed labour market demand to select occupations for certified vocational training pilots for returnees, internally displaced people and eco-migrants. The assessment combined key informant interviews, employer focus groups, a trilingual desk review, a vacancy scan and provider mapping, with validation workshops.

  7. August 2026Events

    Launch of Uzbekistan's revised National Curriculum Framework

    Matthew Goldie-Scot took part, on behalf of UNICEF Uzbekistan, in the launch of the revised National Curriculum Framework in Tashkent. The Ministry of Preschool and School Education and the Republican Education Center developed the framework with technical support from UNICEF and Cambridge University Press & Assessment, with funding from the Global Partnership for Education, the Arab Coordination Group and the Islamic Development Bank.

  8. July 2026Work

    Namibia's first computed national Youth Development Index

    For the Commonwealth Secretariat, Thuso supported Namibia to take its draft youth development framework to its first computed national and regional Youth Development Index, working with a Technical Expert Group, data custodians and the national statistics agency, and training youth data collectors.
