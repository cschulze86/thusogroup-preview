Education sector planning and crisis response with GPE and Education Cannot Wait

Client
UNICEF; Save the Children; CARE; UNESCO; ministries of education
Funding
Global Partnership for Education; Education Cannot Wait
Country
17 countries, and a regional framework for 15 Pacific countries and territories
Years
2016–2025
Support
Systems design; Policy advisory; Evaluation
Sectors
Education; Education in Emergencies

The question

The Global Partnership for Education and Education Cannot Wait fund governments and their partners through structured processes: sector analyses, costed plans that must pass independent appraisal, partnership compacts, joint reviews and multi-year resilience programmes. Each step has its own standards and deadlines. Ministries and grant agents need teams who know those requirements well and can work alongside national planners to meet them.

The work

  • Sector analyses and plans: education sector analyses and strategic plans for Puntland (2016), Kiribati (2020) and Tuvalu (2023–2025); costing and financing of Puntland's 2022–2026 plan; costing, simulation and results frameworks for Vanuatu's 2019–2030 sector strategy; revision of the Federated States of Micronesia's sector plan.
  • Independent appraisals: sector plans for the Maldives (2018), Kiribati (2020) and Papua New Guinea (2025).
  • Compacts and joint reviews: partnership compacts for Uzbekistan (2023) and Tuvalu (2023), and the Uzbekistan compact mid-term review (2025); the Somalia joint review of the education sector (2019); a joint sector review guidebook for Armenia (2023); a results and M&E framework for the Maldives sector plan (2019).
  • GPE-funded programmes: results documentation for Afghanistan's GPE programme (2018); learning assessments and remedial teaching materials for Afghanistan's COVID-19 recovery (2021); GPE COVID-19 accelerated funding applications for Kiribati and the Solomon Islands (2020); curriculum and assessment frameworks in Uzbekistan (2023–2025); a regional all-life skills framework with UNESCO for 15 Pacific countries and territories (2023).
  • Education Cannot Wait multi-year resilience programmes: programme design for northeast Nigeria (2020), Sudan (2021) and the Central African Republic (2022), and review and development support in Somalia (2022); the summative evaluation of the State of Palestine programme, in English and Arabic (2022–2023); a disability assessment for the Iraq programme (2022); an education in emergencies resource mobilisation and advocacy strategy for Nigeria (2022); capacity building for education sector members in the Syria response (2022).

What came out of it

Twenty-nine assignments since 2016: sector analyses, costed plans and simulation models, independent appraisals, compacts and reviews, and multi-year resilience programmes and their evaluations, each delivered to the funder's own standards.

Led by Charles E. Schulze and Matthew Goldie-Scot.
