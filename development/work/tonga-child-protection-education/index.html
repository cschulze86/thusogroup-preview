Child protection in Tonga's schools, from evidence to teacher training

Client
Save the Children Australia
Partners
Ministry of Education and Training, Tonga; Tonga National University
Funding
Australian NGO Cooperation Program
Country
Tonga
Years
2024–2025
Support
Research; Training and learning
Sectors
Child protection; Education

The question

Tonga drafted a National Child Protection Policy in 2022. Save the Children Australia and the Ministry of Education and Training wanted to know where the gaps were in schools, in policy, teacher capacity and children's ability to speak up, and then to give teachers the training to close them.

The work

  • Designed and ran an applied research study on child protection and safeguarding in Tongan primary and secondary schools, guided by a steering group including the Ministry.
  • Turned the findings into a suite of professional development resources: an anti-bullying guidebook, a curriculum, an online course, training-of-trainers materials and orientation workshops, with monitoring and evaluation frameworks.

What came out of it

Tonga's first online course on child protection and child safeguarding for trainee and practising teachers, launched with the Ministry and Tonga National University.

Led by Daniel Cronin.
