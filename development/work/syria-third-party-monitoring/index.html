Independent third-party monitoring in Syria

Client
CARE International; International Rescue Committee; People in Need; Polish Humanitarian Action
Funding
US Bureau for Humanitarian Assistance; FCDO; ECHO
Country
Syria
Years
2020–2024
Support
Monitoring
Sectors
Multi-sector humanitarian response

The question

Access and security constraints in northwest and northeast Syria often prevent agencies from visiting their own programmes. Agencies and their donors needed independent, timely evidence that assistance was reaching the people it was meant for, and to the standard promised.

The work

  • Ran third-party monitoring under framework agreements and rolling contracts for several agencies, covering health, WASH, protection, food security, livelihoods, shelter and cash assistance.
  • Deployed field teams for on-site activity monitoring, post-distribution monitoring, satisfaction surveys, focus groups and key informant interviews.
  • Issued red-flag reports in real time when problems were found.

What came out of it

For CARE alone, 18 monthly monitoring reports between March 2023 and June 2024 across Idleb and Aleppo governorates, with data and tools delivered in Arabic and English.
