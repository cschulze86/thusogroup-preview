Education sector planning and school quality standards in Kiribati

Client
UNICEF Pacific
Funding
Global Partnership for Education; UNICEF
Country
Kiribati
Years
2020–2023
Support
Systems design; Policy advisory
Sectors
Education

The question

Kiribati's schools are spread across remote atolls with limited resources. The Ministry of Education needed a credible sector plan to access Global Partnership for Education funding, and a single framework for judging and improving school quality in place of a set of disconnected tools.

The work

  • Revised and finalised the Education Sector Analysis and the Education Sector Strategic Plan 2020–2023, with a ministerial operational plan, a costing simulation model and an M&E framework, and prepared them for independent appraisal.
  • Extended the Kiribati Quality Standards from early childhood to primary and secondary schools, and piloted them with inspectors, head teachers and teachers.
  • Developed national and school-level education contingency plans.
  • Trained trainers to cascade the quality standards across the system.

What came out of it

A sector plan prepared for independent appraisal and GPE submission, and quality standards for primary and secondary schools, piloted and cascaded through training of trainers.

Led by Matej Damborsky, with the wider Thuso team.
