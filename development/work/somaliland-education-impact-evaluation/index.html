Twelve years of education investment in Somaliland, evaluated

Client
Save the Children Denmark
Partners
CARE International; Ministry of Education and Higher Studies, Somaliland
Funding
European Union; Danida
Country
Somaliland
Years
2024–2025
Support
Evaluation
Sectors
Education; TVET

The question

The Horumarinta Elmiga (HE) programme supported Somaliland's education sector through four phases from 2013 to 2025, across all six administrative regions. Save the Children Denmark asked Thuso to assess what the programme had achieved over its full lifespan: for access, quality, youth employment and the capacity of the Ministry.

The work

  • Designed a multi-phase impact evaluation covering all four programme cycles.
  • Used quasi-experimental methods, including propensity score matching, alongside national EGRA and EGMA learning data.
  • Ran TVET employment and tracer surveys, school assessments, and key informant interviews and focus groups.
  • Facilitated Education System Thinking workshops with Ministry and partner staff.

What came out of it

A final evaluation report, a short version for wider audiences, and a results presentation used for dissemination to Parliament and the Ministry of Education.

Charles Schulze was project lead and lead author, with Matthew Goldie-Scot and Dr Michael Brown as co-authors.
