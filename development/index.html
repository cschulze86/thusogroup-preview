Support

Areas of support

The examples under each area of support are drawn from the list of past assignments and update as new ones are added.

Search all assignments →

  1. Evaluation and monitoring

    Independent evaluations that answer the questions a programme team, donor or board actually has, using methods that fit the context and the budget.

    • Final and mid-term evaluations
    • Impact evaluations
    • Baseline and endline studies
    • Third-party monitoring
    • Evaluability assessments

    Recent examples

    • Aid Fund for Syria (AFS) External EvaluationAdam Smith International · Syria · 2026

    • Horumarinta Elmiga (HE) Education Sector Support Programme: Multi-phase Impact EvaluationSave the Children · Somaliland · 2025
    • Danish TV Livelihoods Programme Final Evaluation: A new chance for vulnerable Syrian children, youth, and young mothers in Northern IraqSave the Children · Iraq · 2024-2025

  2. Research and assessment

    Primary and secondary research to understand needs, markets and systems before money is committed, including in places where access is constrained.

    • Needs assessments
    • Education sector analyses
    • Labour market assessments
    • Surveys and data analysis
    • Situation analyses

    Recent examples

    • Stage 1 Targeted Data Governance Maturity AssessmentCommonwealth Secretariat · United Kingdom · 2026
    • Labour Market Assessment to Inform VET Programme Design in Georgia (REforM)IOM · Georgia · 2026
    • Youth Climate Education Project (ROTA): Capacity-Building Workshops, Pilot Assessment Framework and Final ReportUNDP · Uzbekistan · 2026
    • Commonwealth Secretariat Data Mapping ExerciseCommonwealth Secretariat · United Kingdom, Switzerland, United States · 2025

  3. Policy advisory

    Advice to governments and their partners on policy, legislation, strategy and investment, from the first analysis to a document that can be adopted, funded and put into practice.

    • National policy and legislation
    • Sector plans and independent appraisals
    • GPE partnership compacts
    • Climate and investment policy
    • Governance and data governance

    Recent examples

    • Uzbekistan GPE Partnership Compact Mid-Term Review: Dialogue Facilitation and Memo FinalizationMinistry of Preschool and School Education, Uzbekistan · Uzbekistan · 2025
    • Independent Appraisal of the Papua New Guinea National Education Plan (NEP) 2020-2029UNICEF · Papua New Guinea · 2025
    • Consultancy to Develop the Seychelles Blue Carbon PolicyCommonwealth Secretariat · Seychelles · 2024
    • Development of an Investment Policy for the Tonga Climate Change FundCommonwealth Secretariat · Tonga · 2024

  4. Systems design

    Designing the systems that deliver results at scale: curricula and assessment, quality standards, data and financing systems, and the multi-year programmes that pay for them.

    • Curriculum and assessment reform
    • School quality standards
    • Education financing
    • Skills and TVET systems
    • Multi-year resilience programmes

    Recent examples

    • Youth Climate Education Project (ROTA): Capacity-Building Workshops, Pilot Assessment Framework and Final ReportUNDP · Uzbekistan · 2026

    Courses, curricula and training-of-trainers packages that leave capability behind once an assignment ends, online and in person.

    • E-learning courses
    • Training of trainers
    • Professional development curricula
    • Guidance and toolkits
