The model

Not another platform for everyone

Large-scale software has to serve everyone, so it fits no one closely. These tools work the other way round.

Large-scale softwareThuso
Who it is forAs many users as possibleSmall user bases with specialist needs, in a few well-understood sub-sectors
Who builds itProduct teams far from the workSector experts and developers in one team
SupportHelp articles and a ticket queueConsultancy support from people who do the work themselves
FitYou adapt to the softwareThe software adapts to your frameworks, donors and reports
OwnershipYou rent itUse the tools as they are, or commission and own a bespoke version

The tools carry sector expertise into daily work: the frameworks, checks and standards used on Thuso assignments, available to client teams between visits.

Products

Two tools in early access

Early access

Data Golem

For MEAL teams and evaluators

A monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning platform that takes a team from framework to recurring report. Build or choose a results framework, assemble data collection tools from a bank of modules that calculate indicators automatically, connect your Kobo or SurveyCTO server, and get indicator and evaluation reports drafted on schedule, with every figure checked.

  • Frameworks, indicators and multilingual forms in one place
  • Reports with verified figures, exported to Word and Excel
  • Built on the methods Thuso uses on its own evaluations
Early access

Awrdable

For NGOs and consultants writing bids and grants

An AI-assisted tool that finds funding and contract opportunities, tracks them, and helps you write each application step by step, from reading what is asked to checking the final submission. Pay as you go on prepaid credit.

  • Thousands of open opportunities from public sources, searchable for free
  • A guided path from first read to finished application
  • Your data is never seen by anyone else and never pooled across clients

Ways to work

Three ways to start

  1. Use the tools, with support

    Start with Data Golem or Awrdable, set up by a specialist in the sector and supported as needs change.

  2. Adapt them to your organisation

    Your frameworks, indicators, templates and donor formats built in, so the tool fits the way your team already works.

  3. Commission a bespoke solution

    Sector experts and developers design and build a solution with the client team, then hand it over for the client to own.
